Tip: drag the card left or right, or use the buttons. In Shared expense mode the app auto-detects reimbursements — e.g. an income from Isa that is about 50% of a Mercadona expense — and offers to link them at the right split.

Why happy-balance

Built for real life, not spreadsheets

✨

Smart categorization

Learns from your swipes and auto-tags new transactions by merchant and pattern.

🔗

Shared expenses

Detects reimbursements from a partner at ~50% and links income to the original expense.

📊

Clear dashboard

Cash flow, category breakdowns and balance trends at a glance.

📈

Investment tracking

Tag ETF and stock purchases so saving and investing stay separate from spending.

📥

CSV import

Bring in your bank exports and deduplicate automatically.

🔒

Self-hosted

Runs in your own Docker stack — your data never leaves your machine.
